# DiceBound Beta 0.6.7.13

## Fast Wheel & Slots — #390
- Clearing Board 6 in any mode unlocks a persistent **Fast Wheel & Slots** option.
- The option is hidden until the canonical lifetime Board-clear ledger confirms a Board 6 clear.
- Enabled mode skips Wheel and Slot-machine spin waits while preserving the exact selected result, reward, gameplay state, RNG draw count, RNG order and final RNG state.
- Slots still consume their historical decorative reel RNG draws even when those frames are not painted.

## Combat presentation — #391 + #392
- Combat health labels/bars now sit above the player and enemy presentation.
- Player/enemy models sit slightly lower beneath the health/status stack without moving gameplay targeting.
- The authored battle background tint is 20% more transparent than the 0.6.7.12 baseline, exposing more of the background art.
- Combat View remains the owner of the layout/tint; no gameplay RNG changes are involved.

## Camp difficulty controls — #402
- Nightmare moves exactly 0.1 percentage points left.
- Repository tooling could not reliably inspect the binary campsite art, so the requested fallback was used: the Hell control moves five percentage points right from the previous active-volcano anchor.
- Hell OFF/ON now share one stable anchor instead of the active volcano jumping to a separate location.
- The Devil ritual can no longer be armed by the whole Hell mountain/control.
- A tiny 18px transparent hotspot over the little-devil area is the only ritual activation target; normal Hell-toggle interaction remains separate.
- Bonfire-circle tracking and persistent secret progression are otherwise unchanged.

## Ownership cleanup
- Hell ritual pointer/gesture ownership moved out of `runtime/js/dicebound.js` and into the Camp presentation owner.
- Retired DB24 Hell repaint/ritual listeners and shared Camp ritual CSS were removed.
- Board-clear unlock truth is exposed through the canonical Progression owner.
- Options owns the new setting UI; Road Events owns presentation skipping; Combat View owns combat layout/tint.
- Focused anti-return/deterministic coverage protects the new ownership boundaries.
