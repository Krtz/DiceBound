## Beta 0.6.7.15 — Heirloom Progression

- Renamed the four-rank Legacy **Heirloom Vault** Talent to **Heirloom Loadout** and kept its intended 1→5 next-run carry progression.
- Replaced hidden Prestige-20/60 carry bonuses with three permanent Prestige Moon Loadout ranks costing **1 / 3 / 5 PP**, allowing a complete **8-piece** starting loadout.
- Heirloom Vault remains a permanent **1 PP** unlock with 8 base storage slots. Seven permanent Vault Expansion ranks cost **2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 / 7 / 8 PP** and add **+4 slots each**.
- Board 5, the Road Merchant, the Blood Mage and the Pale Devil each add one permanent Vault slot, for an exact maximum of **40 stored items**.
- Every lifetime Prestige Point earned now adds **+5% to the whole final Legacy XP award per run**, regardless of whether that PP is held or spent.
- Removed the duplicate/dead Legacy **Heirloom Storage** Talent; current careers that still contain it receive its 3 Talent Points back when progression state is repaired.
- Existing old Moon Slot I / Slot II purchase records normalize into the new ranked Vault Expansion without losing their recorded PP spend.
- Extracted Heirloom inventory/Vault mutations into a focused Items owner and moved capacity/rank policy into Progression, draining the touched V24 storage policy and mutation helpers from `dicebound.js`.
- Added deterministic contracts for ranked costs, permanent/refundable semantics, 5→8 loadout progression, 8→40 Vault capacity, milestone slots, lifetime Prestige acceleration, migration and anti-return ownership.
