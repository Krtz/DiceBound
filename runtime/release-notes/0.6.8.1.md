## HUD, Mana and battle presentation hotfixes
- Fixed the Legacy Companion card being able to overlap the combined Character Stats/Gear card in short landscape windows.
- Removed retired responsive placement rules for the old standalone Equipment card and retired the stale `data-sidebar-companion` layout state.
- Two-column short landscape now stacks Character → Companion → Adventure Log safely; three-column short landscape gives Character two columns and Companion one.
- Mana-generator attack buttons/tooltips now read the live resolved Mana gain from the authoritative Mana action owner, including Quick Channel, Summoner bonuses and Invoker generation multipliers.
- Ordinary enemy battle art now grows visibly by 15% per Board: Board 1 = 100%, Board 6 = 175%.
- Wolf and Slime use a 65% family-size baseline while still inheriting Board growth.
- Skeleton, Cultist, Orc and Bandit keep their intentional +15% art calibration inside the same semantic scale policy instead of CSS exceptions.
- Road Merchant, Bloodmage and Pale Devil Secret Boss battle art is 2.5× the former generic guardian size.
- Removed fixed tiered-enemy size rules that silently overrode the semantic scale policy; Combat Presentation now owns battle-art dimensions.
- Added deterministic presentation regressions for live Mana values, Board growth, Wolf/Slime size, Secret Boss size and real Edge Character/Companion overlap.
