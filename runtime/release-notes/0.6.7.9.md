## Beta 0.6.7.9

This release expands #338 into a full secondary-action scaling pass. Paid/signature actions now inherit the builds they are supposed to reward instead of being outgrown by ordinary Attack.

### Mana spenders
- **Sorcerer — Arcane Lance**
  - Still costs 35 Mana and converts 50% of current Echo chance into deterministic bonus damage.
  - Can now Crit.
  - Applies Poison at **Echo chance × Poison chance**.
  - Keeps its guaranteed random core-element eruption and Lifesteal from Lance + eruption damage.
  - The forced eruption continues through the canonical elemental resolver, so **Prismatic Echo** can repeat it.
- **Vampire — Grave Lance**
  - Still costs 35 Mana.
  - Damage now scales with current Lifesteal, converts **80% of Echo chance** into bonus damage, and can Crit.
  - Uses **120% of normal Poison chance** and rolls normal strike elements.
  - Direct + elemental damage heals at **2× current Lifesteal**.
- **Rouge — Scarlet Hex**
  - Still costs 35 Mana, keeps +35% Crit identity and 28% pack splash.
  - Converts 50% of Echo chance into spell damage.
  - Applies Poison at **Echo chance × Poison chance**.
  - Real Rouge drains **2× Lifesteal** from the full primary + splash package.
- **Merchant — Foreclosure Hex**
  - Still costs 40 Mana.
  - Gold contribution is now **5% of current gold with no cap**.
  - Crit and Echo are deterministic damage multipliers instead of Crit rolls.
  - Normal Poison and elemental proc chances remain active.

### Invoker
- Replaces the single mandatory Green builder with three offensive orb attacks:
  - **Quas Strike** — 85% strike potency, uses **70% Echo chance**, forms Blue.
  - **Wex Strike** — 85% strike potency, uses **120% Echo chance**, generates Mana and forms Green.
  - **Exort Strike** — 120% strike potency, uses **70% Echo chance**, forms Red.
- All three use the canonical strike pipeline for Crit, Poison, elements, Lifesteal and ordinary strike effects.
- **Elemental Lance** remains the 50-Mana Red spender, converts 50% of total current Echo chance into deterministic damage, and now also Crits, rolls normal elements, applies Poison at Echo × Poison chance and Lifesteals from direct + elemental damage.
- Guard still forms Blue and Invoke still uses the current three-orb formula.

### Bloodmage, Alchemist, Cleric and Dragoon
- **Exsanguinate** converts 50% of Echo chance into damage, can Crit, rolls normal elements and applies Poison at **(50% Echo chance) × Poison chance**.
- **Volatile Flask** now deals **150% Potion Healing + 100% Attack**, can Crit and uses **250% of current Poison chance** on surviving targets. Existing Alchemist elemental behavior remains.
- **Consecration** can Crit while retaining its heal, Barrier and pack-damage identity.
- **Dragoon Landing** keeps its aerial/landing loop and existing Crit/element behavior and now also uses normal Poison chance.
- Dragoon Jump/Land/cooldown/reset gameplay ownership has been moved out of the monolithic `dicebound.js` body into the Classes action owner.

### Pet classes and Rogue
- **Summoner** and **Beastmaster** now convert a baseline **10% of player Attack into pet damage**.
- Existing class powerups now provide additional Attack-to-pet conversion:
  - Summoner **Deeper Circle**: +10% conversion.
  - Beastmaster **Pack Discipline**: +10% conversion.
- **Rogue — Stat Heist** is a new Rare Unique class powerup. A successful Steal also steals 10% of the target's current ATK and DEF for that battle.
- Stolen Rogue stats are explicitly removed at victory and encounter entry so they cannot leak between battles.
- Pokémon Trainer switching is unchanged.

### Architecture / cleanup
- Secondary-action scaling helpers now live with their canonical Mana/Class/Pet owners rather than accumulating more special-case combat math in `dicebound.js`.
- The old Dragoon gameplay body has been drained from `dicebound.js`; compatibility delegates remain for already-extracted combat owners and regression harnesses.
- Invoker free attacks now use a reusable per-action Echo multiplier instead of a binary Echo suppression flag.

### Design rule
Resource/signature actions should normally be stronger than free Attack when affordable. The strategic choice is whether to spend or save the resource, while Crit, Echo, Poison, elements, Lifesteal, gold, potion healing and pet scaling remain meaningful build investments.

### Validation
Deterministic tests are being expanded across Mana actions, Invoker, bespoke class actions, pet damage, Rogue battle cleanup, presentation and architecture. Full Edge/browser, native WebView2, asset, version and release gates remain required before shipping.
