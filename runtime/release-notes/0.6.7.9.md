## Beta 0.6.7.9

This release begins the #338 class-action balance pass with Invoker and Rouge.

### Invoker — three orb attacks
- Replaces the single mandatory Green-builder attack with three real offensive choices:
  - **Quas Strike** — 85% strike potency, no Echo chain, forms Blue.
  - **Wex Strike** — 85% strike potency, keeps normal Echo/on-hit behavior, generates Mana and forms Green.
  - **Exort Strike** — 120% strike potency, no Echo chain, forms Red.
- Guard still forms Blue, so defensive play remains a valid formula route without being mandatory.
- Elemental Lance remains the 50-Mana Red spender and now converts half of current Echo chance into bonus spell damage.
- All three free orb attacks use the canonical basic-strike pipeline, preserving Crit, Poison, elements, Legendary effects and ordinary strike semantics.
- The retired generic Invoker channel-builder path is removed; Wex Strike is the single owner of Invoker Mana-building attack behavior.

### Rouge — Scarlet Hex
- Scarlet Hex still costs 35 Mana and keeps its high-Crit primary strike plus pack splash.
- Half of current Echo Strike chance is converted into Hex damage, so Echo-heavy Rouge builds improve the spender instead of outgrowing it.
- Real Rouge's doubled Lifesteal now drains from the full Hex damage package: primary plus splash.
- Ordinary Attack is not globally nerfed. The Mana cost remains the tactical reason to save Scarlet Hex for a harder encounter.

### Design rule
Resource spenders should normally be stronger than free attacks when affordable. The strategic choice is whether to spend the resource now or bank it for a later fight, not whether the paid action is a trap.

### Validation
Focused deterministic Invoker, Classes routing, Mana-action, Attack-action, Strike-resolution and Combat-presentation tests cover the changed semantics. Full Edge/browser, native WebView2, architecture, asset and release gates remain required before shipping.
