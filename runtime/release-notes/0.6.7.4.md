## Road traversal footprints

- Every road tile actually crossed by movement now records traversal history in the canonical Board Movement owner.
- Traversal survives active-run checkpoint save/resume and naturally resets with a newly generated Board.
- Board Presentation now owns current/cleared/traversed tile classes instead of duplicating those rules in the composition root.
- Traversed road tiles show a full-tile footprint trail; Merchant tiles are deliberately excluded even when crossed.
- Added deterministic coverage for intermediate/landing traversal, Merchant exclusion, presentation semantics, checkpoint persistence and monolith anti-return ownership.

No Board movement distance, RNG, tile dispatch, event activation, checkpoint schema or Merchant gameplay is intentionally changed.
