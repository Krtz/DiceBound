## Character Gear detail + equipment identity hotfix

### Fixed
- Character Gear no longer leaks literal `<img ...>` markup from old saved Hat icons.
- Hover/focus detail now shows the real equipment identity, rarity, slot, bonuses, Intrinsic, unique effect and set information.
- Impossible Road gear now participates in the modern equipment model: each piece carries a deterministic, slot-valid normal base `equipmentId` with real art and an Intrinsic while retaining its authored Artifact name, unique effect and set layer.
- Axel's Coffee Mug, Kratz Headphones and The Jean Jacket Lost at Kelly's are different by design: each named Mythical is locked to its **own exclusive equipmentId/base identity**, not a randomly rolled ordinary base.
- Those named Mythical identities own their own Intrinsics. The Intrinsic is **additional Mythical power on top of the existing full Mythical bonus package**; no existing authored stats were reduced to pay for it.
- Existing recognized special gear missing its required identity is migrated before equip-stat application so the correct Intrinsic reaches gameplay.
- Current-schema careers with Hat icons contaminated by the retired Beta 0.4.3 presentation bridge are repaired to safe scalar icon data.
- Impossible Road Crown, Kratz Headphones and The Devil's Horns recover semantic scalar fallback icons when affected.

### Equipment model
- Ordinary equipment bases remain eligible through their normal rarity/slot rules.
- Impossible Road deliberately rolls a normal eligible base underneath the Artifact layer, preserving the late-game `Artifact + base + Intrinsic` chase.
- Exclusive named Mythical bases are never eligible for ordinary/base rolls. Axel's Coffee Mug can only use `axels-coffee-mug`, Kratz Headphones can only use `kratz-headphones`, and Kelly's Jean Jacket can only use `kellys-jean-jacket`.
- Their dedicated final art can be attached directly to those fixed identities when the approved binaries land; no architecture rewrite or random base-art fallback is required.

### Cleanup
- Deletes the obsolete `beta043RefreshEquipmentArt()` path that wrote HTML markup into equipment model fields.
- `allBonusesForItem()` now feeds the canonical equip-stat transaction, making modern Intrinsics real gameplay stats rather than comparison/tooltip-only metadata.
- Special identity migration is semantic and bounded; arbitrary old Mythical/Legendary/Omega items are not silently rerolled into random equipment bases.
- Character Gear, Classic Equipment, Heirloom Vault, loot cards and end-of-run gear surfaces share the safe equipment identity/icon path.

### Validation
- Deterministic icon-safety coverage locks markup detection, repair and idempotence.
- Identity tests require exclusive named Mythicals to keep their exact fixed IDs and stay out of every generic roll pool.
- Edge coverage requires the full pre-existing named-Mythical bonus packages to remain unchanged and verifies their Intrinsics add power on top.
- Artifact factory coverage requires every Impossible Road slot to carry a valid modern `equipmentId` with a non-empty Intrinsic without consuming additional gameplay RNG.
- Real Edge coverage reproduces the 0.6.7.29 contaminated Crown failure and requires canonical Crown detail, modern equipment art, live Intrinsic stat application and zero HTML leakage.

No gameplay RNG ordering, save-schema version or equipment-slot rules change. Named Mythicals intentionally gain additional power from their new exclusive-base Intrinsics.

Fixes #455.
