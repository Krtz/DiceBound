## Beta 0.6.7.11

### Luck
- Replaces the previous 100→200 Poor/Common suppression curve with one shared cascading rarity waterfall.
- Every **1 displayed Luck** shifts **0.5 percentage points** of rarity probability upward.
- Luck drains the lowest non-zero tier first, redistributes that mass proportionally across all higher available tiers, then continues into the next tier after the lower one reaches zero.
- Baseline ordinary gear remains 55% Poor at 0 Luck, so **110 Luck makes Poor exactly 0%** before the waterfall begins draining Common.
- Generated gear, guardian ordinary gear and Powerup weighting use the same semantic policy.
- Rarity selection keeps the existing one-roll RNG contract; zero-weight tiers cannot win on an exact zero RNG boundary.

### Enemy variety
- Temporarily gives Boards 2–6 the same ordinary enemy-family selection window/progression as Board 1.
- Removes later-Board family-floor offsets and Board 6's hidden +12/+14 selection offsets.
- Board-specific stats, Ringbound/Abyssal presentation, pack rules, guardians, events and difficulty scaling remain unchanged.
- This is an interim policy while a richer per-Board roster design is worked out under #393.

### Camp
- Moves the Nightmare toggle another **5 percentage points down**, from `x .89 / y .48` to `x .89 / y .53`.
- Hell positioning is unchanged.

### Validation
- Adds deterministic Luck waterfall coverage for gear, guardian loot and Powerups.
- Adds Board-generation coverage proving every Road currently uses the Board 1 family-selection window.
- Updates Camp layout characterization for the new Nightmare anchor.
