# DiceBound Class Lore

> Narrative companion to `docs/STORY_BIBLE.md` and issue #94.
>
> **Status:** WORKING THEORY unless a section explicitly says otherwise. These entries are intended to give every current playable class a place in the setting without locking every mystery too early.

---

# The Ways

The Road does not understand people the way people understand themselves.

It remembers patterns.

When a pattern becomes stable enough across possibility, the Road can reconstruct it as a **Way**: a shape a Dicebound traveler can step into and temporarily make true.

A Way is not merely a profession.

It can be a discipline, a survival strategy, a relationship, a curse, a joke repeated often enough to become law, or an idea that reality has failed to forget.

The traveler does not necessarily become a different biological person every time they choose a class. Instead, the Road reorganizes the traveler around a remembered possibility:

> **Ah. I remember someone like this.**

Class unlock requirements are therefore accidental rituals of remembrance.

The player thinks they met a gameplay requirement.

The Road thinks they supplied evidence.

Some Ways are easy for reality to remember. Others require repeated contradiction, impossible statistics, secret encounters, or relationships strong enough to survive erased timelines.

Secret Ways should feel especially unsettling: they are not necessarily stronger because they are hidden, but because the Road had reasons not to offer them casually.

# Ranger — The Way of the Marked Horizon

**CORE DIRECTION**

Ranger is the easiest heroic shape for the Road to remember.

There were hunters before there were kingdoms, accountants, gods, guilds or formal schools of magic. Across countless realities, somebody stood at the edge of safety, studied something dangerous from a distance, and decided exactly where the next arrow needed to go.

That repetition gave the Ranger unusual stability. It is one of the few Ways the traveler can inhabit immediately because the Road does not need to be reminded what a hunter is.

The Ranger's **Marked Quarry** is more than practiced aim. Each mark is a narrowing of possibility. The target is observed, measured and increasingly denied futures in which the next shot misses. Arrow Storm is the final collapse: several arrows, several possible trajectories, all made true quickly enough that the Road cannot comfortably separate them.

The Ranger is therefore a quiet introduction to DiceBound's deeper metaphysics. At first it looks like mundane skill.

Later the player may realize that extreme precision and probability manipulation are cousins.

### Why it is always unlocked

The Road remembers hunters even when it forgets civilizations.

Ranger is what remains when history is stripped down to **traveler, danger, distance, choice**.

# Sorcerer — The Way of Borrowed Stars

**WORKING THEORY**

Sorcery is what happens when somebody notices that the sky above the Road is not always the same sky.

The earliest Sorcerers learned that stars which technically did not exist could still exert influence if remembered strongly enough. They stopped asking whether a constellation was present and began asking whether some possible world had ever believed it was.

Mana is the pressure created by holding those impossible correspondences inside one body.

Channel Bolt gathers that pressure. Arcane Lance releases it. Starfall is the dangerous conclusion of the practice: calling down a celestial event from a sky the current Road may never have possessed.

Sorcerers are often accused of borrowing power from elsewhere.

The more worrying possibility is that there may no longer be an "elsewhere."

### Unlock meaning — defeat the Board 1 miniboss

The Ogre Roadwarden is the traveler's first encounter with something that appears to possess **authority over the Road**.

Defeating it is the first proof that the rules of the route are not absolute.

The Road responds by remembering a Way built around the same heresy:

> Rules can be reached from outside.

# Fighter — The Way of Standing Ground

**CORE DIRECTION**

The Fighter is not remembered because swords are common.

The Fighter is remembered because refusal is common.

Across realities, walls failed, kingdoms ended, monsters grew larger, and somebody still planted their feet and said:

> No further.

That act became stable enough to survive the collapse of worlds.

Counterstance embodies the principle perfectly. The Fighter does not merely endure an attack; Guard turns the enemy's certainty into stored violence. Titan Cleave extends that refusal outward, cutting through several threats while building a Barrier from the act of surviving them.

The Fighter Way is one of the Road's most human memories: not invulnerability, not destiny, simply the stubborn decision to still be present after something tried to remove you.

### Unlock meaning — defeat the Board 1 final boss

The Ancient Road Dragon is the first thing on the journey that truly feels older and larger than the traveler.

Defeating it proves that persistence can overcome age, scale and apparent inevitability.

The Road remembers the Fighter because the traveler has demonstrated the oldest Fighter ritual:

**stand somewhere impossible to stand, and remain.**

# Monk — The Way of Unbroken Motion

**WORKING THEORY**

Most Ways impose themselves on the Road.

The Monk cooperates with it just enough to become dangerous.

Old martial traditions discovered that actions performed in perfect sequence begin to acquire momentum beyond muscle. One strike makes the next more likely. One dodge creates the angle for another. Motion becomes expectation, expectation becomes probability, and probability becomes a path the Road is reluctant to interrupt.

This is the origin of the Monk's Flowing Combo.

The combo is not a magical counter hovering above the universe. It represents an unbroken causal sentence. So long as the Monk continues attacking, reality keeps finishing the thought. Guarding, stopping to drink, or otherwise breaking rhythm ends the sentence and forces the Road to begin again.

Hundred Fists is what happens when the distinction between "one action" and "many actions" becomes temporarily negotiable.

### Unlock meaning — defeat the Board 2 miniboss

By the second Road, the traveler has seen that strength alone is not enough. The Titan Guard is a lesson in scale and structure.

Defeating it teaches the Road that the traveler can win through continuity rather than brute force.

The Way of Unbroken Motion becomes available.

# Clown — The Way of Weaponized Nonsense

**CORE DIRECTION**

Scholars have tried to prove that the Clown Way is a reconstruction error.

The Clown Way has responded to this academic criticism with pies.

Across enough realities, performance, humiliation, improvisation, misdirection, danger and laughter became entangled with survival. Court fools survived rulers who killed generals. Performers crossed wars under banners nobody considered military. Tricksters escaped systems by refusing to behave like the kind of problem those systems knew how to solve.

Eventually the Road began remembering **comedy as a combat doctrine**.

Opening Gag is reality reaching into that accumulated tradition and selecting whichever ridiculous advantage seems funniest at the time. Final Punchline is the same principle without restraint: the battlefield briefly obeys dramatic structure instead of tactical sense.

The terrifying thing about the Clown is not that the universe is joking.

It is that, statistically, **the joke worked often enough to become an archetype**.

### Unlock meaning — defeat the Board 2 final boss

By defeating the Astral Devourer, the traveler survives a creature tied to cosmic impossibility.

The Road responds with an important philosophical correction:

Cosmic horror is not the only valid reaction to an incomprehensible universe.

Sometimes you honk.

# Rouge — The Way of Living Crimson

**WORKING THEORY**

Rouge is the colour.

This distinction has survived the death of several libraries and is apparently important enough that the Road itself insists upon it.

The Rouge Way comes from realities where art was not a representation of magic but one of its practical languages. Pigment carried intention. Shape constrained force. Repetition created symbols. Red, in particular, accumulated meanings that were difficult to separate: blood, warning, love, violence, heat, ceremony, shame, beauty.

A Rouge does not cast a spell and then paint it.

The painting is the spell.

Crimson Stroke builds Mana by making increasingly forceful marks on reality. Scarlet Hex weaponizes the accumulated composition. Crimson Deluge is the moment the canvas stops pretending to be metaphorical.

Its innate relationship with Lifesteal suggests the oldest crimson schools may have discovered that blood is uniquely resistant to being treated as an abstract symbol.

### Unlock meaning — reach 10 Prestige

Prestige repeatedly destroys a lived route while preserving fragments of its meaning.

After enough Prestiges, the traveler has effectively painted over reality again and again without fully removing the earlier layers.

The Road recognizes the technique.

It remembers Rouge.

# Berserker — The Way of Wounds Remembering

**CORE DIRECTION**

Pain is one of the Road's most reliable records.

Worlds disagree about gods, kings, mathematics and morality.

Bodies are more consistent about being hurt.

The Berserker Way emerges from people who learned to turn that reliability into power. Damage becomes proof of continued existence. Missing blood, broken armor and failing breath are not merely disadvantages; they are evidence that the Berserker has already survived the thing currently trying to kill them.

Blood Rage converts absence into force. The closer the Berserker comes to being erased, the louder their remaining existence becomes.

This is why Rage scales with missing life rather than simply with anger.

The Berserker is not powered by emotion.

The Berserker is powered by **contradiction**:

> I should be dead. I am not. Therefore something must give.

Ragequake makes the battlefield give.

### Unlock meaning — take 1,000 total damage

The Road only remembers the Berserker after the traveler has supplied enough accumulated evidence that suffering is not successfully removing them.

At that point, pain stops being merely something that happened.

It becomes a Way.

# Turtle — The Way of the Last Wall

**CORE DIRECTION**

Some worlds built fortresses.

Some bred guardians.

Some discovered, apparently, that the most elegant defensive architecture was a turtle.

The Turtle Way is the stable idea of **protection becoming identity**. Where the Fighter chooses to stand ground, Turtle becomes the ground's refusal to move.

Shell Discipline turns patience into retaliation. Barriers accumulate around a creature already built as a barrier. Defense ceases to be a number describing armor and begins to function like a metaphysical statement:

> This thing is still here.

The Road dislikes statements that remain true for too long.

Turtle is very good at making them.

### Unlock meaning — exceed 40 Defense in a run

At sufficiently absurd Defense, the traveler stops merely wearing protection.

They demonstrate the concept of **fortification strongly enough to become biological**.

The Road remembers Turtle.

Nobody knows why the remembered form is specifically a turtle.

The Road declines questions.

# Frog — The Way of the Second Impact

**WORKING THEORY**

The Frog Way is one of the strongest pieces of evidence that the Great Reduction had quality-control problems.

It is also, unfortunately, extremely effective.

Somewhere across possibility, predatory amphibians, martial doctrine, elastic movement and repeated strikes converged into a stable heroic pattern. The Road remembers the result with complete confidence and no visible embarrassment.

Frog specializes in Echo because its Way treats an action as incomplete until it has bounced, repeated or returned. A tongue strike is not merely an attack. It is the first point in a sequence. Croak Cascade turns the battlefield into a chain of valid landing points, each one creating the possibility of another.

At extreme Echo values, Frog begins to demonstrate something deeper about the Road: repetition can become so certain that the "extra" action is no longer meaningfully extra.

It is simply what happened next.

### Unlock meaning — exceed 150% Echo Strike in a run

The traveler repeats actions so aggressively that one attack reliably contains more than one attack.

The Road searches its damaged memory for an archetype capable of explaining this behavior.

It finds Frog.

This raises further questions.

# The Twenty-Sider — The Way Beyond Six

**CORE DIRECTION / MAJOR MYSTERY**

The Dicebound die has six faces.

The Road appears to prefer sixfold structures.

There are six Boards. Six results are normally enough to choose the next local reality.

The Twenty-Sider is therefore not merely whimsical.

It is alarming.

This Way represents a traveler capable of perceiving or imposing a much larger field of outcomes than the Road normally exposes. Combat actions become visible d20 rolls because the class does not hide its metaphysics behind swordsmanship, spellcraft or instinct. It reaches directly into uncertainty and asks for more options.

A natural twenty is not "good luck."

It is a moment when an outcome with extremely low local permission is nevertheless selected as true.

A natural one is the opposite: the cost of opening too many nearby possibilities at once.

The Twenty-Sider may be one of the closest existing classes to understanding what the Dicebound die actually is.

### Unlock meaning — raise DiBo to level 30

DiBo's connection to the impossible die remains unresolved.

The working theory is that a sufficiently strong bond with DiBo teaches the traveler that **six outcomes were never the real limit**.

Once learned, the Road cannot make the traveler forget that fact.

# Slime — The Way Without Shape

**CORE DIRECTION**

Every ordinary Way asks the traveler to become something.

Slime is what becomes possible after the traveler has become almost everything else.

A slime has no fixed posture, no inherited weapon discipline, no requirement that yesterday's shape resemble today's. It survives through adaptation. The Road, which desperately wants stable categories, finds this philosophically offensive.

That is why Slime is so good at borrowing.

It is not copying classes in the scholarly sense. It is demonstrating that a Way may be treated as texture rather than identity: absorb a pattern, deform around it, keep what works.

Poison and strange survivability fit the same principle. Slime wins by refusing to maintain the boundaries other creatures consider necessary.

### Unlock meaning — unlock every non-secret class

Only after the traveler has taught the Road a broad catalogue of stable Ways can the opposite idea become visible:

> What if the stable pattern is having no stable pattern?

The Road remembers Slime.

Or perhaps Slime was waiting for the Road to run out of excuses.

# Vampire — The Way of Borrowed Blood

**WORKING THEORY**

Vampires discovered a brutal solution to continuity.

If the Road erases what belongs only to you, then survive using something that belonged to someone else.

Lifesteal is not merely healing in the Vampire tradition. It is appropriation of persistence. Blood, vitality, warmth and memory are taken from another body and used as evidence that the Vampire should continue existing.

This may be why vampirism persists across so many incompatible realities. It is an extraordinarily effective strategy in a universe where identity itself can fail.

The Vampire's occult Mana practices suggest that later blood traditions learned to treat stolen vitality as both nourishment and spell medium.

A sufficiently old Vampire may not know which parts of them were originally theirs.

### Unlock meaning — exceed 100% Lifesteal in a run

At that point the traveler has demonstrated an impossible principle:

They can take back more life than a blow should reasonably return.

The Road recognizes the pattern as predation elevated into continuity.

It remembers Vampire.

# Ninja — The Way Between Moments

**WORKING THEORY**

The Ninja Way is built around actions the Road notices too late.

Crit is normally treated as exceptional precision: one attack finding a particularly meaningful opening. At extreme levels, that explanation breaks down. The opening cannot plausibly be exceptional if it happens every time.

Ninja is what the Road remembers when precision becomes **temporal theft**.

The strike lands in the version of the moment where the defense was already wrong. Smoke is less about hiding from eyesight and more about making the Road briefly uncertain which position belongs to the attacker.

Repeated critical hits therefore build the Ninja's advantage. Each one is evidence that the traveler is acting from a more favorable version of the present than their enemy.

The more this happens, the harder the battlefield finds it to keep the Ninja properly located.

### Unlock meaning — exceed 100% Crit in a run

When every qualifying attack has become critical certainty, the traveler has stopped "finding weaknesses."

They have learned to occupy the instant in which weakness is guaranteed.

The Road remembers Ninja.

# CEO — The Way of Executive Mandate

**CORE DIRECTION, REGRETTABLY**

The existence of the CEO Way has caused several historians to abandon their professions.

It implies that across enough realities, corporate authority became such a stable mechanism of power that the Road now recognizes it alongside martial arts, sorcery and vampirism.

CEO does not create value.

CEO creates **claims about value**, and the universe has apparently suffered enough bureaucracy to take those claims seriously.

Boss Damage is particularly appropriate. The CEO Way specializes in identifying large concentrations of authority, labeling them an obstacle to quarterly performance, and making that classification mechanically relevant.

Executive Compensation's absurd Gold multiplication may be evidence that wealth is not purely economic on the Road. Enough people believing that an executive deserves more can become a causal force.

This is perhaps the bleakest lore in the game.

### Unlock meaning — reach extreme Boss Damage in a run

The traveler demonstrates a sustained ability to treat powerful guardians as targets for optimization rather than mythic threats.

The Road recognizes management potential.

Humanity is not consulted.

# Merchant — The Way of the Price That Survives

**CORE DIRECTION / MAJOR MYSTERY**

The Road Merchant is suspicious because commerce persists remarkably well across broken realities.

Currencies change. Nations disappear. Histories contradict one another.

Yet someone always understands the sentence:

> What will you give me for it?

The Merchant Way is built around that terrifying continuity.

Occult Accounting treats value as a kind of magic because, on the Road, it may genuinely be one. Selling, buying, debt, profit and foreclosure are promises about future possibility. A price states that two unlike things may be made equivalent. Enough agreement makes the equivalence real.

The Road Merchant seems to understand this better than almost anyone.

The playable Merchant may therefore be less a profession and more a **license**.

### Unlock meaning — defeat the Road Merchant five times

Killing the Road Merchant once proves the Merchant can die.

Killing them repeatedly and continuing to meet them proves something much stranger:

**the office survives the officeholder.**

Eventually the traveler has participated in the cycle often enough that the Road recognizes them as part of the same institution.

The Merchant Way opens.

Whether the Road Merchant considers this theft, promotion or onboarding is unknown.

# Cleric — The Way of Witnessed Mercy

**WORKING THEORY**

Faith on the Road is complicated by the fact that entire religions may originate from worlds that no longer exist.

Clerics solve this problem pragmatically.

They begin with the act rather than the doctrine.

A wound closes. A dying companion stands. A Barrier holds. Somebody expected mercy and received it.

Repeated often enough, these events become evidence that **something answers**.

Whether that something is a surviving god, a remembered god, the Camp, collective expectation, the Road itself, or an ancient mechanism designed to respond to devotion should remain unresolved for a long time.

The Cleric Way is therefore not proof that one theology is correct.

It is proof that care can become causally stubborn.

### Unlock meaning — heal 1,000 total HP

The traveler has restored enough lost life that healing itself becomes part of their persistent identity.

The Road remembers a Way built around refusing to accept injury as final.

It remembers Cleric.

# Paladin — The Way of the Kept Oath

**CORE DIRECTION**

Paladin does not appear merely because the traveler learns to fight and heal.

It appears when those two traditions become one promise.

The Fighter says:

> I will stand.

The Cleric says:

> I will restore.

The Paladin says:

> **I will remain responsible for what happens here.**

That difference matters.

Oaths are exceptionally powerful on the Road because they are statements intended to remain true across changing circumstances. A kept oath is therefore a tiny act of resistance against a reality built from revision.

Faith, Guard, Defense and healing converge naturally in the Paladin because all four are forms of continuity imposed by commitment.

### Unlock meaning — clear Board 3 with Fighter and Cleric

The Road must see both parent traditions survive the Fractured Road before it accepts their synthesis.

Only then does it remember the Paladin.

A Paladin is not merely armored faith.

It is **two Ways agreeing not to contradict each other**.

On the Road, that is almost miraculous.

# Beastmaster — The Way of the Pack

**CORE DIRECTION**

Most classes are reconstructed from an individual archetype.

Beastmaster is reconstructed from a relationship.

Pets are unusually resistant to route collapse. They remember commands, affection and shared danger after events the Road insists never occurred. A sufficiently bonded group becomes a network of mutual recognition stronger than any one member's memory.

The Beastmaster Way emerges when the traveler learns to participate in that network rather than merely own companions.

Switching pets, pack stances and command effects are different expressions of the same idea: the Beastmaster's unit of identity is not one body.

It is **the pack**.

This may be why Beastmasters are especially difficult for the Road to erase cleanly. Removing one participant does not remove the others' memory of them.

### Unlock meaning — unlock every pet

The traveler has established contact with every currently remembered companion type.

At that point, the Road can no longer interpret the relationships as isolated accidents.

It recognizes a Way built from the entire network.

# Rogue — The Way of Temporary Ownership

**WORKING THEORY**

Rogue and Rouge are unrelated.

The Road knows this.

Please stop filing corrections.

The Rogue Way comes from people who noticed that ownership is one of reality's softer rules.

A sword is "yours" until somebody takes it. Gold belongs to a king until a thief leaves through a window. Locked doors make claims about who may pass. Rogues specialize in testing those claims and discovering how many are merely social suggestions wearing metal hardware.

Stealing therefore has deeper significance on the Road. An object transferred without permission has still changed hands. Reality tends to accept possession faster than morality does.

The Rogue's affinity for wealth and evasion follows naturally: acquire possibility, avoid the consequences, repeat until the distinction between fortune and theft becomes academic.

### Unlock meaning — reach 4,000 Gold in a run

The traveler accumulates enough portable value that wealth itself becomes a tactical environment.

The Road remembers the sort of person who looks at that environment and immediately wonders how much of it is actually supervised.

Rogue becomes available.

# Bloodmage — The Way Written in Flesh

**CORE DIRECTION / MAJOR MYSTERY**

Bloodmage is not learned safely.

The traveler does not discover it through study, practice or ordinary progression.

They kill the Bloodmage.

Something remains.

Blood magic treats the body as a record. Wounds are notation. Life is fuel. Lifesteal is revision. A spell paid for in blood cannot be dismissed as purely symbolic because the payment physically occurred.

This makes blood unusually useful in a world built around disputed history.

A scar can survive an argument about whether the battle happened.

The Bloodmage secret boss may have learned to exploit this principle deliberately, preserving continuity inside flesh while the Road rewrites everything around it.

The playable Bloodmage Way raises an uncomfortable possibility:

The traveler may not have unlocked it.

They may have been **infected with a method of remembering**.

### Unlock meaning — defeat the Bloodmage secret boss

The fight creates direct contact with a form of continuity the Road does not normally offer.

When the Bloodmage dies, the method survives in the traveler.

Whether this was accidental should remain unresolved.

# Summoner — The Way of the Open Door

**WORKING THEORY**

The Summoner begins with a dangerous observation:

Not everything the Road forgot is gone.

Some possibilities remain nearby as pressure, instinct, dream, spirit or unfinished shape. Most people experience these remnants as déjà vu, nightmares or the feeling that a place should contain something it does not.

Summoners answer the feeling.

Mana opens the route. Spirits cross it. Conjure gives the visitor enough definition to act. Pets and summoned entities fit naturally together because both rely on relationships that can persist despite unstable reality.

A careless Summoner asks what can be called.

A wise Summoner asks what remembers being invited.

### Unlock meaning — raise three pets to level 10

Three mature companion bonds provide repeated proof that stable relationships can bridge route resets.

The traveler learns the shape of that bridge.

The Road remembers Summoner.

# Pokémon Trainer — The Way of Six Companions

**WORKING THEORY / PROTOTYPE NAME**

The current class name is prototype shorthand and is tracked for future originalization under #93. The lore should therefore be built around DiceBound's own **six-companion trainer archetype**, not around external franchise mythology.

Where Beastmaster becomes part of a pack, the Trainer Way becomes a **curator of possibilities**.

Different companions are answers to different situations. The Trainer does not ask one creature to become universally perfect. They maintain a roster, understand each relationship, and select which possibility should stand at the front right now.

The six-creature draft is especially interesting in-setting. Six is the Road's preferred local number of possibilities. A Trainer carrying six companions may therefore resemble the Dicebound die more closely than anyone realizes: six living options, one made active, the others still present as unrealized support.

Assists suggest the roster does not fully accept the Road's demand that only one choice be active at a time.

### Unlock meaning — raise every pet to level 10 and clear Board 5 Nightmare with Beastmaster

This is not ordinary animal handling.

The traveler must demonstrate deep continuity with every companion type, then carry the philosophy of the pack through one of the Road's most self-aware and recursive regions under Nightmare pressure.

Only then does the Road remember the Way of Six Companions.

A future public-safe class name should preserve this identity.

# Alchemist — The Way of Repeatable Miracles

**CORE DIRECTION**

Sorcery asks reality to make an exception.

Alchemy asks reality to do the same thing twice.

That distinction made Alchemists dangerous long before the Road broke.

A potion is powerful not merely because it heals. It is powerful because somebody can write down ingredients, repeat a procedure, and obtain approximately the same impossible result tomorrow. In a world where history itself is unstable, reproducibility approaches rebellion.

The Alchemist Way treats the battlefield as a laboratory with unacceptable safety standards. Repeated attacks brew resources. Potions become both medicine and Volatile Flasks. Potion Healing is not just recovery scaling; it represents the practitioner's growing mastery over how efficiently a mixture persuades biology to change state.

The best Alchemists are offended by the word magic.

The worst Sorcerers find this hilarious.

### Unlock meaning — use 25 potions across all runs

The Road observes the traveler applying the same class of intervention across multiple routes and repeatedly obtaining survival.

Eventually it recognizes the pattern as more than consumption.

It remembers a practitioner.

# Ouroboros — The Way That Returns to Itself

**CORE DIRECTION / MAJOR MYSTERY**

Ouroboros is one of the Ways most closely aligned with the actual structure of the Road.

Echo says an action may happen again.

Ouroboros asks a more dangerous question:

> What if the second action causes the first?

Its serpent imagery is not merely decorative. The closed loop is a model of causality without a clean beginning. Poison feeds damage, damage feeds repetition, repetition creates more opportunities for the state that produced the repetition. The class grows strongest when effects stop behaving like a line and begin behaving like a circle.

This is disturbingly similar to Prestige.

It is also disturbingly similar to the Road.

The Ring Tyrant and other Board 5 entities may react differently to an Ouroboros-shaped traveler because the class is imitating principles those guardians were built to contain.

### Unlock meaning — reach 400% Echo Strike in a run

At that point, repetition has exceeded any reasonable interpretation of a follow-up attack.

The traveler has demonstrated an action so recursive that "again" becomes the default state.

The Road remembers Ouroboros.

Or recognizes it.

Those are not necessarily the same thing.

# Slime Rouge — The Misremembered Way

**CORE DIRECTION / SECRET CLASS**

Slime Rouge is what happens when the Road is forced to answer a question it does not have a category for.

The traveler first learns Slime: identity without fixed shape.

Then, while using Random, they reach the end of Board 6 without committing to one stable Way.

The Road is asked to remember someone who has already proven that form is optional **and** that class identity can remain unresolved all the way to the edge of the Last Road.

The answer comes back red.

Why red is unclear.

Rouge scholars insist this is significant.
Rogue scholars insist it is theft.
Slimes decline to participate in the debate.

Slime Rouge begins each run with a borrowed passive and a borrowed Ultimate that may not belong together. This is not merely randomness. It is the Road reconstructing a class from mismatched memories and discovering, to its annoyance, that the result still functions.

The Way therefore represents **successful misclassification**.

It is a living proof that the Road's categories are conveniences rather than laws.

### Unlock meaning — unlock Slime, then clear Board 6 with Random

The ritual combines two impossibilities:

- a body that refuses fixed form;
- a journey that refuses fixed identity.

At the deepest currently traversable Road, that contradiction becomes stable enough to persist.

Slime Rouge is remembered.

Probably incorrectly.

# Cross-class lore principles

These should guide future class dialogue, unlock screens, achievements and story reveals.

## 1. Unlock text should feel like recognition

Avoid framing every unlock as a bureaucratic game reward.

Player-facing flavor can increasingly imply that the Road has **noticed a pattern**.

Examples:

> **A Way has been remembered: Berserker.**
>
> Pain failed to remove you often enough that reality learned the lesson.

or:

> **A Way has been remembered: Turtle.**
>
> At some point protection stopped being equipment and became anatomy.

Exact UI wording can remain shorter.

## 2. Not every Way needs one historical founder

Some may come from one legendary individual.

Others may be composite archetypes reconstructed from thousands of similar people across incompatible worlds.

Leaving this variable makes the setting larger and stranger.

## 3. Class selection is alignment, not costume change

The Dicebound traveler is still the traveler.

Choosing a Way lets one remembered pattern temporarily dominate how reality interprets them.

This supports future modular player visuals under #89: the body can remain recognizably the Dicebound traveler while stance, equipment, effects and class-specific features express the active Way.

## 4. Secret Ways should reveal something

Secret classes should ideally teach the player a small truth about the setting:

- Twenty-Sider: six outcomes are not the real limit.
- Merchant: institutions/value can survive their individual holders.
- Bloodmage: flesh can preserve continuity.
- Slime Rouge: class categories themselves are not fundamental.
- other future secret Ways should expose similarly interesting cracks in the Road.

## 5. Absurdity is evidence

Do not apologize for Frog, Turtle, Clown, CEO or future nonsense classes.

The more confidently the Road treats an absurd Way as legitimate, the more it reinforces the central mystery:

**What kinds of realities had to exist for this idea to become stable enough to survive the end of worlds?**
