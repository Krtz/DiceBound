## Memory Cache

Board 4+ Treasure can become a Memory Cache. In Beta 0.6 it produces generated Legendary equipment and preferentially rolls a Legendary Effect the career has not discovered yet.

|---|---:|
| Normal | 1 / 450 (0.2222%) |
| Nightmare | 1 / 300 (0.3333%) |
| Hell | 1 / 200 (0.5%) |

## Ordinary guardian equipment

Miniboss ordinary-equipment chance is **85% Normal / 92% Nightmare / 100% Hell**. Final bosses still guarantee an ordinary equipment item. Nightmare and Hell each have a 25% chance to promote the rolled ordinary rarity one step; Hell therefore can receive both promotions because Hell includes Nightmare.

## Generated Legendary Effects

Beta 0.6 launches with 20 effects:

1. **Twin Surge** — Arcane Surge hits twice at 70% damage per hit.
2. **Sword and Shield** — powerup Attack gains also grant Defense, and Defense gains also grant Attack.
3. **Perfect Specimen** — Ouroboros stabilizes at 30 Attack before excess Attack converts to Echo.
4. **Echo Chamber** — Crit chance converts one-for-one into Echo chance during attacks.
5. **Critical Feedback** — critical Echo Strikes grant 8 Ultimate.
6. **Blood Price** — Exsanguinate gains +15% damage and builds +8% battle damage per use.
7. **Glass Fortress** — Defense counts double against incoming damage; max HP is reduced by 30% while equipped.
8. **Second Barrel** — Gun elemental procs repeat at 65% elemental power.
9. **Elemental Roulette** — each non-Echo basic strike guarantees one random elemental proc.
10. **Prismatic Weapon** — a weapon-element activation also erupts all six DiBo core elements at 40% power.
11. **Loaded Sixes** — a movement roll totaling exactly 6 gains +6 additional movement.
12. **Last Stand** — once per battle, lethal damage leaves 25% HP and grants 3 Barriers.
13. **Vampire's Bargain** — Lifesteal above 100% becomes equal bonus strike damage.
14. **Iron Echo** — damaging Echoes grant +1 Defense for the battle.
15. **Recursive Poison** — after Poison ticks, each surviving poisoned enemy has 35% chance to gain another stack.
16. **Perfect Guard** — Guard counter damage can Echo using current Echo chance.
17. **Hoarder's Arsenal** — every 500 gold adds +1 damage to basic/Echo strikes.
18. **Unstable Ultimate** — Ultimate can be used at 70 charge and deals 75% normal damage.
19. **Pet Mirror** — after the companion attacks, 25% chance to repeat the latest elemental proc at 65% power.
20. **Reverse Engineering** — Attack and Defense granted by equipped gear swap places.

Class-specific effects only enter the effect pool when compatible with the active class identity.
